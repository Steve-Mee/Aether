### Sprint 6 — Ultimate Autonomy
- `SelfEvolvingCodebase.py` — AI die eigen modules analyseert en refactoring voorstelt
- `AgenticCommerce.py` — Customer AI agents + Merchant AI agents die onderhandelen

### Sprint 7 — IPO & Physical
- `IPOPrep.py` — Board structuur, compliance checks, financial reporting
