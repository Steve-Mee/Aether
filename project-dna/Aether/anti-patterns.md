# Anti-Patterns

These are patterns we actively avoid in the AETHER project.

## Design Anti-Patterns

- Adding features or complexity without clear merchant value.
- Building configuration-heavy systems instead of smart defaults.
- Treating the AI layer as a secondary helper instead of a central orchestrator.
- Creating tight coupling between the AI layer and specific business logic.
- Sacrificing long-term simplicity for short-term feature delivery.

## Process Anti-Patterns

- Making changes without considering impact on autonomy and simplicity.
- Allowing technical debt that makes future evolution harder.
- Ignoring merchant perspective in architecture and feature decisions.
- Over-engineering solutions when a simpler approach would suffice.
- Bypassing Plan Mode for significant architectural or cross-cutting changes.

## Experience Anti-Patterns

- Creating interfaces that feel busy or overwhelming.
- Adding options and settings that increase cognitive load.
- Building systems that require constant human oversight for routine operations.
- Prioritizing visual "premium" feel over actual calm and speed.
