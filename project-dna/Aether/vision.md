# Vision

AETHER is an AI-native merchant operating system built on the principle of radical simplicity. Its purpose is to give merchants powerful, autonomous tools while delivering a calm, premium, and reliable experience.

## Core Ambition
To build an intelligent operating system that allows merchants to run significant parts of their business with minimal daily oversight, supported by a highly capable AI orchestration layer.

## Definition of Success
- Merchants experience genuine autonomy and reduced operational burden.
- The system feels calm, fast, and intentional (luxury through simplicity).
- The AI layer handles routine work reliably and intelligently.
- Complexity is minimized; every feature must justify its existence by delivering clear merchant value.
- Long-term sustainability and merchant success are prioritized over short-term feature addition.

## Long-term Direction
AETHER should evolve into a highly autonomous merchant platform where intelligence, simplicity, and reliability reinforce each other. It is not a traditional e-commerce platform with AI bolted on — it is an AI-first operating system designed for merchants.
